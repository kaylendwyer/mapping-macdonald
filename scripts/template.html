Reading the marks. A solid mark is a date the editors gave plainly; a hollow, dashed mark is one they bracketed as estimated — a month, a season, a year, or a choice between days. Estimated entries are placed at the earliest possible date. Cancelled engagements are drawn at reduced opacity. Six entries have no recorded location and appear in the timeline and the table but not on the map.

Types. The article’s twelve labels collapse to four: lecture, performance, reading, sermon. Two entries are both a lecture and a sermon and appear under either filter. Readings and sermons share one colour on the map and timeline (five entries between them); the exact type is named in the tooltip and the table. Twenty entries had no type in the source, or one the editors bracketed, and are marked inferred.

Places are geocoded from OpenStreetMap; four are regions rather than towns and sit at the region centroid. Source spellings were resolved against each entry’s own citations — see macdonald_places.csv for every judgement call.
